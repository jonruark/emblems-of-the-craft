# Emblems of the Craft

*A Masonic adventure in 8 bits.*

Emblems of the Craft is a retro, Game Boy style creature-collecting RPG set in a world shaped by the symbols of Freemasonry. You play a seeker who has just knocked on the door of the Craft. With a living Emblem at your side, you travel from Ashlar Vale toward the Lodge of the First Light, and from darkness toward light.

**Current release:** 1.1, Chapter One: *The Entered Apprentice*

---

## Quick Start

1. Open `emblems of the craft 1.1.html` in any modern browser (Chrome, Edge, Firefox, Safari).
2. Click the game screen once so it can receive keyboard input.
3. Press **Enter** at the title screen and choose **NEW GAME**.

It needs no install, no server and no internet connection. The whole game is one HTML file.

## How to Play

### The basics
- **Explore** towns and roads, talk to everyone, and read signs. Press A while facing a person or object.
- **Wild Emblems** hide in tall grass. Walking through it can start a battle.
- **Trainers** will challenge you if you walk into their line of sight.
- **Refreshment Halls** fully heal your Emblems for free. The last Hall you used is where you wake up if your whole party faints.

### Battles
Battles are turn-based. On each turn choose one of:
- **FIGHT:** use one of your Emblem's moves (up to four).
- **BAG:** use an item, or present a Tracing Board to a wild Emblem.
- **EMBLEM:** switch to another member of your party.
- **RUN:** escape from a wild battle. You cannot run from a trial.

When you open the move list, a small colored square next to a move tells you how well it will work against the current foe: **green** for super effective, **red** for not very effective.

### Catching Emblems
Weaken a wild Emblem first, then choose **BAG > TRACING BOARD**. The lower its HP, the more likely it is to join you. You can carry six Emblems. Any more go to the **Secretary's archives** in a Refreshment Hall, where you can deposit or withdraw them.

### Growth
Emblems earn EXP by winning battles. As they level up they learn new moves, and some of them **evolve**. When an Emblem with four moves wants to learn a fifth, you choose which move to forget, or you can decline the new one.

### Saving
Open the menu (Enter) and choose **SAVE** to have the Secretary record your progress in the minutes. Saves are stored in your browser on that device. Choose **CONTINUE** on the title screen to pick up where you left off. The game also saves automatically when you complete the degree.

## The Three Pillars (Types)

Every Emblem belongs to one of the three great pillars of the lodge:

| Type | Pillar | Strong against | Weak against |
|---|---|---|---|
| **WISDOM** | Ionic | Strength | Beauty |
| **STRENGTH** | Doric | Beauty | Wisdom |
| **BEAUTY** | Corinthian | Wisdom | Strength |

Moves of the neutral **CRAFT** type hit every Emblem for normal damage. A move that matches its user's type gets a 50% bonus.

## Items

| Item | Price | Effect |
|---|---|---|
| Tracing Board | 60 | Present it to a weakened wild Emblem, and it may choose to join you. |
| Corn | 40 | The corn of nourishment. Restores 25 HP. |

## The Menu

- **EMBLEMS:** view your party, read each Emblem's summary, and change the order.
- **CODEX:** a record of every Emblem you have seen and befriended.
- **BAG:** your items.
- **TOOLS:** your Apprentice Card, with play time and the working tools you have earned.
- **SAVE**, **SOUND** and **EXIT**.

## Chapter One Walkthrough

<details>
<summary>Spoilers: click to expand</summary>

1. **Ashlar Vale.** Wake at home and visit Brother Euclid's study, the blue-roofed house by the pond. Choose your starter from the trestleboard.
2. **Rival battle.** Cowan, a know-it-all who learned everything from books, takes the Emblem with the type advantage over yours and challenges you. Win or lose, the story goes on.
3. **The Quarry Road.** Euclid gives you Tracing Boards. Go north past the Tyler and catch new Emblems in the tall grass. Watch for Apprentice Tom, Stonecutter Mae, three hidden items, and a second battle with Cowan near the top of the road.
4. **Pillar Gate.** Pass between the pillars Boaz and Jachin. The ruffian **Ignara** blocks the lodge door and fights with a Wisdom-type Emblem, so a Beauty type is a good answer. Visit the old Past Master's cottage for a gift.
5. **The Lodge of the First Light.** Get past the Inner Guard and the Senior and Junior Deacons, then challenge **Junior Warden Corinth** in the South. His team is Beauty-heavy, so Strength types shine here.
6. **The East.** The Worshipful Master confers the First Degree and presents the working tools of an Entered Apprentice.

**Recommended level** for the Junior Warden: 12 or higher.

## About the Masonic Content

The game uses symbols, lore and language from **published, public sources**, such as monitorial lectures, the tracing boards and the Hiramic legend. That includes the three pillars, the working tools, the rough and perfect ashlar, Boaz and Jachin, the mosaic pavement, the blazing star, and the stations of the lodge officers.

**No modes of recognition, passwords or other private ritual content appear in the game.** It is a light-hearted tribute, written to be enjoyable for brethren and non-Masons alike.

## Technical Notes

- **One file, no dependencies.** All graphics are drawn in code at runtime, and all music and sound effects are synthesized with the Web Audio API. Nothing loads from the network.
- **Resolution:** 256 x 224 internal canvas, scaled up with crisp pixel edges to fit the window.
- **Font:** Press Start 2P by CodeMan38, embedded as a data URI and used under the SIL Open Font License 1.1.
- **Storage:** your save and your sound setting are kept in the browser's localStorage. Clearing site data for the file erases them. If localStorage is unavailable, the game still plays but cannot save.
- **Audio:** browsers start sound only after your first key press or tap, so the title music begins once you interact.

### Source layout (development build)

The release file is assembled from these sources by `build.py`:

| File | Contents |
|---|---|
| `src/shell.html` | Page shell, styles, touch controls |
| `src/core.js` | Canvas, input, audio, music sequencer, font, dialog and menu system |
| `src/art.js` | Tiles, character sprites, Emblem artwork |
| `src/data.js` | Types, moves, Emblems, items, songs |
| `src/world.js` | Game state, maps, movement, NPCs, rendering |
| `src/battle.js` | Battle engine, catching, leveling, evolution |
| `src/ui.js` | Menus, party, bag, shop, codex, title, intro |
| `src/content.js` | Chapter One maps, characters and story scripts |
| `src/main.js` | Main loop and startup |

To build: `python3 build.py "emblems of the craft 1.1.html"`
